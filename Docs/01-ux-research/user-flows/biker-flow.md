- **Response Time**: <2 minutes from job offer to acceptance

- **Success Rate**: >98% successful pickup completion
- **Customer Satisfaction**: >4.7/5 driver rating of biker service
- **Safety Record**: Zero accidents, consistent safety protocol adherence

### Earnings Optimization:
- **Hourly Rate**: $25-35/hour including tips and bonuses
- **Daily Targets**: 12-20 successful pickups during peak hours
- **Geographic Efficiency**: Maximum earnings per mile traveled
- **Batch Completion**: 80% of offered batch jobs completed successfully
- **Platform Rating**: Maintain top-tier status for priority job access
